How booking works

Four steps from the home page to a boarding pass.

📝

1. Register

Give us your traveller details once. They are saved to your profile for every future booking.

🔐

2. Sign in

Use your username or email. We check the credentials against your stored account before letting you through.

🔎

3. Search and book

Pick a route and date, choose a cabin, and confirm. You get a six-character PNR straight away.

🎫

4. Manage the trip

View every booking on your dashboard, cancel a seat if plans change, and watch your SkyMiles add up.
